# Tee Watch – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 9, 2026_

Tee Watch has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer collects nothing and runs no server for the app.

**What stays on your phone:** the name of the GitHub repository you point the app at, the GitHub token you enter
(if any), your notification setting, and a list of tee times you've already been notified about. Uninstalling the
app deletes all of it.

**Who the app talks to:**
- **GitHub** (api.github.com, raw.githubusercontent.com), to read and change the watch list in the repository you
  chose. What's in that repository (days watched, search area, tee times found) is stored by GitHub under your
  account and is visible to anyone if the repository is public. Your token is sent only to GitHub.
- **Golf course and tee-time sites**, only when you tap a tee time: the booking page opens in your browser.
- **Your phone's location lookup (Android Geocoder)**, only when you type a city or ZIP for the search area. The
  result (latitude and longitude) is saved to your repository. The app never reads your phone's location.

**Notifications** are created on the phone from the data above; nothing is sent to the developer.

**Children:** the app isn't directed at children under 13.

**Contact:** open an issue at https://github.com/lostak/golf-watcher/issues.
